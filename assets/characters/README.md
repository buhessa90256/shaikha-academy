# Shaikha's character assets

Six original character poses were generated with the built-in image-generation tool from the user-supplied master character reference. The source photograph is excluded from this repository. Transparent alpha is preserved. Shaikha appears on the home islands, every activity header, parent-guide pages and reward screens.

The welcome pose used the master reference for Shaikha's identity and an existing sister-site character only for the matching storybook art style. The remaining poses use Shaikha's generated welcome character as the consistency reference.

Use case: illustration-story. Create a single full-body transparent preschool learning-game mascot of SHAIKHA. Image 1 is Shaikha's identity, clothing, hair and accessory reference. Image 2 is ONLY the desired soft polished 3D storybook cartoon art style used by her sister's learning app; do NOT copy that girl's face, loose hair or striped dress. Preserve Shaikha's own recognizable round face, dark brown eyes, warm light-brown skin, dark curly hair in a high bun with loose forehead curls, large ivory flower hair accessory, small gold earrings, ivory embroidered long-sleeve jacket over her matching knee-length ivory dress, white lace socks/tights and ivory shoes with silver toe caps. She remains a young child with natural child proportions. Pose: facing viewer, warm happy smile, waving hello with one hand and holding a big yellow learning pencil upright in the other. Entire body and props visible, centered, modest transparent margin. Rounded kid-friendly shapes, soft warm shading, readable silhouette at small app size. Genuine transparent alpha background, no scenery or colored backdrop, no text, no logo, no other people. Exactly one character in one welcome pose.
